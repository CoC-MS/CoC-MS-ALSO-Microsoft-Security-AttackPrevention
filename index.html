A starting point, not a complete security solution.

These recommended templates are a good starting point. Protection depends on enabling and enforcing them across all intended users, resources, and devices, not just a small pilot group. A limited assignment only protects that group. Test and roll out in stages, then expand to the full appropriate scope. Keep admin-only policies targeted to admins, review exclusions and emergency-access accounts, and check licensing, prerequisites, and policy conflicts.
