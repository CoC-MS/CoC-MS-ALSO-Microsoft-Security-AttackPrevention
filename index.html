How to read this map

Each association is derived from the actual grant controls, authentication-flow conditions, risk levels, locations, or session settings in a pinned source snapshot. Expand a policy to see the evidence, rationale, and original targeting. Names alone never establish coverage. Templates without a reviewed association stay visible in the catalog; they are not assumed ineffective.

Technique-to-tactic classifications follow the linked MITRE ATT&CK references. Tactic totals count distinct policy codes with at least one association, not a percentage of ATT&CK coverage. A scenario describes a possible attack path, not an assertion that every attack uses every listed technique. There is no direct CA association here for encryption, compute hijacking, phishing delivery, or arbitrary RDP lateral movement.

Package labels identify source folders, not independent licensing advice. Check the source repository guidance and Microsoft requirements. Review emergency access accounts, exclusions, named locations, groups, grant operators, and application scope before deploying templates. This navigator never creates, edits, enables, or deploys policies.
